# Fracture-Risk-reduction-using-RL-and-XAI
Fracture risk assessment remains a critical public health challenge, predomi-nantly constrained by the reliance on expensive diagnostic modalities such as dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry (DXA). This study presents a novel framework integrating reinforcement learning (RL) and explainable AI (XAI) to mitigate fracture risk by targeting micro-trauma, defined as the monthly frequency of minor stumbles or near-falls, as a predictive indicator. Data from 120 participants (targeting 200) were collected via a Google Forms sur-vey, capturing lifestyle variables (e.g., physical activity, calcium intake, sleep duration) and micro-trauma metrics, achieving a 100% micro-trauma reduc-tion in most cases (average 13.6 ± 5.2 weeks), with rare residuals due to ini-tial conditions

A Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) model, trained over 20,000 timesteps, optimized interventions across a 50-week simulation, achieving a 100% mi-cro-trauma reduction (average 13.6 ± 5.2 weeks). Five-fold cross-validation yielded an average validation reward of 2.2875 ± 0.975, affirming robust-ness. SHAP analysis reveals Activity (mean|SHAP| ~0.40) as the primary driver, with Age (0.15) and Micro Trauma (0.10) notable, while Calcium, Sleep, Gender, and Smoking (<0.05) show minimal impact, reflecting the re-ward design. This accessible, interpretable approach outperforms BMD-centric tools like FRAX, with future plans for 200+ participants, clinical tri-als, and mobile deployment.
